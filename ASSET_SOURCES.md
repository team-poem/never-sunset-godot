## Original work

All world geometry, procedural textures/materials, prop models, Korean narrative adaptation, and UI authored for this project.
Five deterministic WAV assets generated by tools/make_audio.py; no third-party recordings or samples.
Six original 256 × 256 surface PNGs in assets/surfaces are generated by tools/make_surface_maps.py: plaster, wood, and tile color/normal pairs. No third-party image inputs. These generated texture assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
