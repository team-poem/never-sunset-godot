## Original work

All world geometry, prop models, Korean narrative adaptation, and UI were authored for this project. Six original deterministic WAV assets are generated by tools/make_audio.py. The single-beat pulse-beat.wav uses the same original synthesis formula as pulse.wav, and the wall animation supplies its timing. The original ambient sounds are mixed with the recordings below. The six earlier procedural PNG prototypes remain in assets/surfaces for reference; the Web export excludes them and the final apartment uses the ambientCG maps below.

## ambientCG surfaces — CC0 1.0

Nine unchanged 1K JPG files provide Color, OpenGL Normal, and Roughness maps:

| Asset | Actual use | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plaster001 | Painted interior walls and trim, restrained normal strength | https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plaster001 |
| WoodFloor051 | Floorboards and wood furniture | https://ambientcg.com/view?id=WoodFloor051 |
| Tiles133A | Bathroom and kitchen ceramic tiles | https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tiles133A |

Files live in `assets/vendor/ambientcg/<asset>/`. Archives were obtained from `https://ambientcg.com/get?file=<asset>_1K-JPG.zip`, checked for ZIP integrity, and visually inspected before use. License declaration: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/ . CC0 legal text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode . A source/license notice is in assets/vendor/ambientcg/LICENSE.txt and the Web license bundle.

The initial PaintedPlaster010 and Tiles074 candidates were not used: their exposed brick and grand checkerboard styling did not fit an inhabited apartment.

## Kenney recordings — CC0 1.0

27 unchanged OGG recordings are in `assets/vendor/kenney/`:

- **RPG Audio**, Kenney Vleugels: cloth1–4, creak1–2, bookOpen, bookClose, bookFlip1, metalClick, metalLatch, metalPot1–2, doorOpen_1, doorClose_1. Used for curtain pulls, paper, switches, the weighted drain cover and doors. Official page: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio . Official archive: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/rpg-audio/8e99002d76-1677590336/kenney_rpg-audio.zip .
- **Impact Sounds**, Kenney: footstep_wood_000–003, footstep_concrete_000–002, footstep_carpet_000–001, impactGlass_light_000–001, impactWood_light_000. Used for material-specific footsteps, cups and quiet contact sounds. Official page: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds . Official archive: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/impact-sounds/87b4ddecda-1677589768/kenney_impact-sounds.zip .

Both official archives include CC0 notices permitting personal and commercial use. The license wording is preserved beside each pack; only line endings and trailing whitespace were normalized. Both notices are included in the exported THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt. No Freesound file was obtained or redistributed. No paid, account-gated, or non-redistributable assets are used.
